📚 Detailed Guides

▶ 🎵 Soundboard Features

Playing Sounds

  • • Click to Play: Just click any sound card on the dashboard
  • • Queue System: Sounds are queued automatically if one is already playing
  • • Waveform Preview: Visualize audio before playing
  • • Favorites: Star sounds to keep them at the top of your list

Uploading Custom Sounds

  • • Login required (use the 🔐 Login button)
  • • Supported formats: MP3, WAV, OGG, OPUS
  • • Max file size: 5MB per sound
  • • Optionally upload images for thumbnails
  • • Auto-conversion to optimized formats (Opus/WebP)

Filtering & Search

  • • Guild Filter: Filter sounds by server
  • • Search Bar: Find sounds by name or tags
  • • Global Sounds: Available across all servers
▶ 🎶 Entrance Sounds

Entrance sounds play automatically when you join a voice channel. Choose from 4 different modes:

1. Single Sound

Pick one sound that always plays when you join.

2. Random Pool

Select multiple sounds and the bot will randomly pick one each time you join.

3. Combo Sequence

Queue multiple sounds to play in order with custom delays between each.

4. Scheduled Themes

Set different entrance sounds for specific days/times (e.g., "Friday Party Mode").

💡 Tip: Go to your Profile to configure your entrance sounds!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

▶ Why can't I play sounds?

Make sure you're in a voice channel! The bot needs to join your voice channel to play sounds.

▶ How do I upload custom sounds?

You need to be logged in. Click the 🔐 Login button, authenticate with Discord, then you'll see an Upload button on the dashboard. Supported formats: MP3, WAV, OGG, OPUS (max 5MB).

▶ Do entrance sounds work across all servers?

Yes! Your entrance sound is global and will play in any server where the bot is present, as long as you join a voice channel.

▶ How do scheduled entrance sounds work?

Scheduled entrances let you set different sounds for specific days and time ranges. For example, you can have a "Friday Night" theme that only plays on Fridays between 6 PM and midnight. Configure this in your Profile.

▶ Can admins override my entrance sound?

Yes, server admins can override entrance sounds for specific users. If your entrance is overridden, you'll see a warning banner in your Profile page. You can still change your preference, but the admin's choice will take priority.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues

Bot not joining voice channel

→ Make sure you're in a voice channel

Sounds not playing

→ Check if the bot is connected to voice. Try /beeps to test playback

Upload fails

→ Ensure file is under 5MB and in supported format (MP3, WAV, OGG, OPUS, PNG, JPG, GIF)

Can't access admin pages

→ You must be logged in AND have admin permissions in the server.

Dashboard won't load

→ Try clearing your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R) or use incognito mode
